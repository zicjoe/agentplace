# M16 Stage 2A — Production acceptance

**No fresh paid research required.** Use existing signed-in state and the Smart Money Scout SOL conversation from M16 Stage 1.

1. **My Workers:** From navigation select Workers. Confirm title **My Workers**, one installed card per Worker, worker status, last relevant Job where available, Add Worker to Discover, and no false creator labels. Refresh to confirm account-scoped persistence.
2. **Continue:** Find Smart Money Scout. If its Worker conversation has messages, its primary action should say **Continue work**. Open it and confirm the existing SOL conversation and research answer are present. Back returns to My Workers. No duplicate conversation or new Job should be created by navigation.
3. **Work history:** Expand Smart Money Scout's Work history. Open the completed SOL Job; see existing evidence. Return via browser/app navigation. Supporting Worker contributions should not be presented as approvals that the specialist owns.
4. **Live status:** If a Worker is currently running a Job, it appears under Working; completed jobs never by themselves make a Worker Working. Needs-approval/needs-you Jobs appear under Needs you for their lead/origin Worker. A paused Worker appears under Paused and opens a reviewable Worker workspace, without automatically resuming it.
5. **Filters and responsive UI:** Confirm All/Working/Needs you/Paused counts and empty-filter state. Check a narrow/mobile viewport and keyboard focus access.
6. **Account boundary:** Sign out and verify My Workers does not expose the prior account's Worker names or Job titles. Signing in again must restore only that owner's persisted records.
7. **Safety:** No signing, execution approval, new Job, paid request or authority grant occurs from merely opening a Worker or inspecting a Job. Financial permissions remain separate.
